Clickflow is the ultimate in-page multi-step sequence recorder tailored specifically for developers, QA testers, and power users who frequently need to perform repetitive tasks like logging out, navigating specific flows, or resetting test environments.

Tired of manually clicking through the same exact menus and buttons over and over again? Clickflow streamlines your workflow by injecting a sleek, non-intrusive floating widget directly onto any webpage, letting you record your clicks and replay them instantly.

Key Features:
• Quick Sequence Execution: Run your saved automated flows instantly. Click the extension icon or use the `Alt+X` shortcut to automatically execute a sequence of clicks and navigations without lifting your hands from the keyboard.
• Smart Recording Engine: Visually record the elements you click on any website to map your multi-step sequence effortlessly. Support for complex CSS selectors ensures robust playback.
• Secure Local Storage: Your data stays with you. All recorded sequences are saved securely within your browser's local extension storage. We do not track, collect, or transmit your data to any external servers.
• Import & Export: Easily back up your recorded sequences to a JSON file and import them across different devices or testing environments using the dedicated Options page.
• Modern, Lightweight UI: Designed with a beautiful, responsive, and intuitive interface that uses Shadow DOM to prevent any styling conflicts with the websites you are interacting with.
• Keyboard Shortcuts: Force open the widget with `Alt+V` to view or edit sequences, and execute them instantly with `Alt+X`.

Whether you are automating a multi-step sign-out process, managing test environment resets, or just need a faster way to navigate complex web apps, Clickflow offers a premium, bloat-free experience that stays out of your way until you need it.

How to Use:
1. Open the Clickflow widget using `Alt+V` to start recording a sequence on the current page.
2. Click on the elements you want to automate in the page to add them to your flow.
3. Save the sequence, then simply click the extension icon or use `Alt+X` to replay the sequence instantly.
4. Right-click the extension icon and select "Options" to manage, export, or import your sequences.
